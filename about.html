About EcoWise

Leading the E‑Waste Revolution

We are a team of environmental advocates and tech experts committed to secure, sustainable e‑waste recycling.

Our Mission

Zero Waste, Full Circle

At EcoWise, we believe that yesterday's electronics are tomorrow's resources. Our mission is to provide secure, transparent, and eco‑friendly e‑waste recycling that protects your data and the planet.

We partner with certified facilities to recover precious metals, plastics, and rare earth elements, keeping them out of landfills and back into the supply chain.
