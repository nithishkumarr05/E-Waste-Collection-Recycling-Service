How It Works

Our Recycling Process

Every item is handled with care through our 4‑step system.

1

Secure Intake & Sorting

Items are logged, sorted by type, and prepared for data destruction.

2

Data Destruction

Certified wiping and physical shredding for all storage devices.

3

Material Recovery

Precious metals, plastics, and glass are extracted for reuse.

4

Certified Reporting

You receive a detailed certificate of recycling and data destruction.
